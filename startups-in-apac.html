Why Engineering Velocity Matters in Asia-Pacific

The APAC startup ecosystem presents unique opportunities for investors who track engineering velocity signals. With 24 tracked teams across 13 sectors, the region shows high variance in individual team output — Vacademy-io's 697 commits in 14 days and aipoch's 307 demonstrate that APAC teams can rival global velocity leaders despite smaller average team sizes.

The E-commerce Infrastructure sector is particularly strong in APAC, with startups like juspay (65 contributors, 100-commit velocity), bagisto, and beikeshop building mission-critical payment and commerce infrastructure. Healthcare also features prominently with ohcnetwork and earthians showing 100 and 86 contributors respectively — large teams by APAC standards. Framework Migration dominates at 12 teams, suggesting broad technology stack modernization across the region.

Methodology: How Engineering Velocity Data Is Collected

GitDealFlow monitors public GitHub activity across 324 venture-backed startups globally. For each startup, we track commit velocity, contributor growth rates, repository expansion, and signal type classifications (Deploy Spikes, Hiring Bursts, Infra Buildouts, Framework Migrations) using a trailing 60-day analysis window. Our methodology is published on SSRN and peer-reviewed. Data is refreshed weekly every Sunday. The 24 APAC startups tracked here represent all venture-backed engineering organizations in the region that meet our inclusion criteria of minimum 10 public repositories and 5 active contributors.
