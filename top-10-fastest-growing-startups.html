About This List

This ranking tracks the 10 startups with the largest engineering velocity acceleration in Q3 2026. Velocity change is measured as the percentage difference in commit activity between the trailing 14 days and the prior 14-day period. A high velocity change often precedes product launches, fundraise announcements, or major team expansions — making this list a leading indicator for deal flow sourcing.

The current #1, fleetbase (Supply Chain, Pre-seed), shows a +1,600% velocity surge with 17 commits from 7 contributors. PhotoFlare also records +1,600%. Eight of the top 10 show +999% growth. The startups span 8 sectors including Healthcare, EdTech, Data Infrastructure, Supply Chain, Fintech, and Enterprise SaaS — across geographies from the US to Europe and APAC. Most accelerations come from small teams (3-48 contributors) experiencing rapid growth phases.

How to Use This Data

The top 10 fastest-growing list is designed as a deal-flow sourcing tool for venture investors and startup scouts. A startup appearing on this list with a +999% or +1,600% velocity change is typically in one of three scenarios: (1) preparing for a fundraise by accelerating product development, (2) responding to a significant new customer or partnership that requires rapid feature delivery, or (3) emerging from stealth with a burst of initial engineering activity. In all three cases, the signal often precedes public announcements by 3-6 weeks.

To get the most value from this list: cross-reference each startup with the Tools Hub for deeper profiles, use the Velocity Checker for real-time lookups, and check the Scout Score tool to validate your sourcing instincts. For recurring updates, subscribe to the weekly signals digest at /subscribe.

Methodology: GitDealFlow computes engineering velocity as total commits across all public repositories in a startup's GitHub organization over a trailing 14-day window. Velocity change is the percentage difference between the current 14-day window and the prior 14-day window. All values are computed from publicly observable GitHub data and updated weekly. Full methodology at signals.gitdealflow.com/methodology.
