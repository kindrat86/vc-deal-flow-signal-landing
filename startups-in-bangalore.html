Why Bangalore?

Bangalore is India's Silicon Valley and the startup capital of Asia-Pacific's fastest-growing major economy. The city hosts the largest concentration of engineering talent outside the United States, feeding a vibrant ecosystem spanning SaaS, fintech, edtech, and enterprise infrastructure. Home to unicorns like Byju's, Swiggy, and Razorpay, Bangalore's startup scene benefits from deep pools of software engineering talent, a mature VC landscape (Accel, Sequoia India, Peak XV), and a massive domestic market as a testing ground.
