Your Early-Stage Edge

Angel investing is pattern recognition. The most successful angels identify breakout teams before the metrics are obvious — before the revenue numbers, before the press coverage, before the institutional VCs come calling.

Engineering velocity is the earliest quantitative signal available. Unlike revenue (often zero at pre-seed), user growth (often manipulated), or press mentions (lagging), GitHub activity is real-time, transparent, and predictive. Our validated panel shows that velocity acceleration precedes fundraising events by 21–47 days.

For angel investors, this means you can build a data-informed pipeline of early-stage startups to watch, track their engineering momentum over time, and reach out at the right moment — before the deck is circulating.

How to Use Scout Score

The Scout Score is your angel investing report card. Enter any GitHub username at gitdealflow.com/scout to get a 0–100 score based on how early that user starred startups that later became unicorns.

Score 80+ (Elite Scout)

You consistently star breakout startups before they hit 500 GitHub stars. Your pattern recognition is exceptional.

Score 50–79 (Active Scout)

You have a good track record of early discovery. Follow more early-stage repos to sharpen your signal.

Score below 50 (Developing)

You're early in your angel investing journey. Use the Trending This Week board to practice spotting accelerating startups.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can angel investors use engineering velocity data? ▼

Angel investors can use GitDealFlow to discover pre-seed and seed-stage startups before they start fundraising. By monitoring engineering velocity — commit frequency, contributor growth, and deploy cadence — angels can identify teams that are building product-market fit before the metrics are visible in revenue or user growth data.

What is the Scout Score and how do angels use it? ▼

The Scout Score is a 0–100 metric that measures how early a GitHub user discovers breakout startups. Angels use it to validate their own pattern recognition — a high Scout Score means you've consistently starred startups before they became widely known. It's a quantifiable measure of your angel investing instinct.

Do you track pre-seed and seed startups specifically? ▼

Yes. GitDealFlow tracks 83 pre-seed and 85 seed-stage startups in its dataset. These early-stage companies often show the strongest correlation between engineering velocity changes and future fundraising events.
