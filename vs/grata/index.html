GitDealFlow vs Grata

Bottom line up front: Grata answers “find me companies that do X”; GitDealFlow answers “which of them is heating up right now.”

Grata is a B2B company-search engine built for M&A sourcing and private-equity deal discovery. GitDealFlow is a pre-round signal tool — it reads startups’ public GitHub engineering activity and flags the ones accelerating — 21–47 days before the round. it does not store funding-round histories, valuation tables, investor profiles, or contact data — it is a leading indicator, not a database.

What Grata does well

Grata earns its place in the stack as a B2B company-search engine built for M&A sourcing and private-equity deal discovery. Its real strengths:

  • Best-in-class B2B company search by function, technology, and niche
  • Purpose-built for PE/M&A sourcing workflows
  • Covers the long tail of small private companies databases miss

Where GitDealFlow is different

GitDealFlow is not a broader version of Grata — it is a different kind of instrument. Grata records what already happened. GitDealFlow flags what is about to. The data is public GitHub activity (commit velocity, contributor growth, repository expansion) across 4,200+ startup orgs in 20 sectors, and the acceleration patterns it surfaces have historically preceded fundraise announcements by 21–47 days before the fundraise announcement.

Concretely, that means GitDealFlow will show you a startup accelerating before Grata has a round on file for it. it does not store funding-round histories, valuation tables, investor profiles, or contact data — it is a leading indicator, not a database.

  • No GitHub or engineering-activity signal
  • No fundraise timing — Grata finds companies, not momentum
  • Geared toward PE buyout sourcing, not early-stage VC signal
Important: Grata is not wrong or bad — it answers a different question. Most serious deal-flow teams run both: a database for the record, and a signal for the lead.

The verdict

Choose GitDealFlow if your problem is finding startups before the round. Grata answers “find me companies that do X”; GitDealFlow answers “which of them is heating up right now.”

Use both: Use Grata to build a long list, then GitDealFlow to rank who is accelerating.

Common questions

Is GitDealFlow a Grata alternative for M&A sourcing?

No — GitDealFlow is a momentum signal, not a B2B search engine. Grata finds companies by function; GitDealFlow detects which are accelerating on GitHub.

Pricing context

GitDealFlow ranges from a permanent free Signal Digest (5 startups every Sunday, no card) to a €9.97/month dashboard and a €97/month Insider Circle. Grata is enterprise pricing, sold to PE and M&A teams. For individual angels and small funds, the gap is large; for institutional desks, it reflects very different products.
