GitDealFlow vs Private Equity Wire

Bottom line up front: Private Equity Wire covers fund-level news; GitDealFlow covers startup-level pre-round signal.

Private Equity Wire is a PE/VC news and fund-data wire — LP/GP news, fund announcements, and performance data. GitDealFlow is a pre-round signal tool — it reads startups’ public GitHub engineering activity and flags the ones accelerating — 21–47 days before the round. it does not store funding-round histories, valuation tables, investor profiles, or contact data — it is a leading indicator, not a database.

What Private Equity Wire does well

Private Equity Wire earns its place in the stack as a PE/VC news and fund-data wire — LP/GP news, fund announcements, and performance data. Its real strengths:

  • Timely PE/VC fund-level news and LP/GP announcements
  • Fund performance and fundraising data
  • Editorial quality and industry reach

Where GitDealFlow is different

GitDealFlow is not a broader version of Private Equity Wire — it is a different kind of instrument. Private Equity Wire records what already happened. GitDealFlow flags what is about to. The data is public GitHub activity (commit velocity, contributor growth, repository expansion) across 4,200+ startup orgs in 20 sectors, and the acceleration patterns it surfaces have historically preceded fundraise announcements by 21–47 days before the fundraise announcement.

Concretely, that means GitDealFlow will show you a startup accelerating before Private Equity Wire has a round on file for it. it does not store funding-round histories, valuation tables, investor profiles, or contact data — it is a leading indicator, not a database.

  • News-driven — reports after the fact
  • Fund-level, not startup-level signal
  • No engineering or momentum data
Important: Private Equity Wire is not wrong or bad — it answers a different question. Most serious deal-flow teams run both: a database for the record, and a signal for the lead.

The verdict

Choose GitDealFlow if your problem is finding startups before the round. Private Equity Wire covers fund-level news; GitDealFlow covers startup-level pre-round signal.
