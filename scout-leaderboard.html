  • Coverage — How many unicorns did you spot early?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Scout Score?
Scout Score is a 0-100 rating that measures how well your GitHub starring history aligns with validated unicorn startup outcomes. It reveals your invisible talent for spotting breakout companies before they raise.
How is my score calculated?
We analyze your public GitHub starring history against ~75 validated unicorn startups. The algorithm scores timing (how early you starred them), coverage (how many you spotted), and pattern recognition (alignment with breakout trajectories).
Is it really free?
Yes! Completely free. No sign-up, no credit card. Just enter your GitHub username and get your score instantly.
How do I get on the leaderboard?
Check your Scout Score at /scout and share it on Twitter with the hashtag #ScoutScore. High-scoring users appear on this leaderboard automatically.
