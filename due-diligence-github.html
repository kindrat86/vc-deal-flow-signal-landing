GitHub in Due Diligence: 5 Engineering Metrics Every Investor Should Check

Published 2026-07-30 · By The Data Nerd · 9 min read

Before writing a check, most investors review financials and team backgrounds. Few check the dataset that reveals how the team actually builds: public GitHub activity. Here are 5 engineering metrics from the GitDealFlow methodology for your due diligence checklist.

1. Commit Velocity Trend

Are they shipping more code? A declining commit velocity before a fundraise is a red flag. fleetbase shows ++1600%.

2. Contributor Diversity

How many different engineers are committing? A team of 3 matching a team of 10 may indicate bus-factor risk. fleetbase: 7 contributors.

3. Repository Architecture

Number and structure of repos reveals engineering maturity. Startups with 3+ well-structured repos show infrastructure awareness.

4. Issue Resolution Velocity

Fast issue resolution correlates with customer responsiveness. High-velocity startups close 80%+ of issues within a sprint.

5. CI/CD and Automation Maturity

CI/CD configurations and test coverage indicate engineering maturity. Automated testing means reliable scaling post-fundraise.
