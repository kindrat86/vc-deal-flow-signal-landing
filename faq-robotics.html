FAQ: Robotics Startup Signals — Everything Investors Need to Know

Robotics startups are building the hardware and software that automate physical work — from industrial arms and warehouse bots to surgical robots and autonomous vehicles. GitDealFlow tracks 28 robotics companies through their public GitHub engineering activity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Robotics Startups

How does GitDealFlow measure engineering velocity in robotics?
Robotics engineering velocity is measured from the public GitHub activity of 28 robotics startups, covering their software stacks (ROS, simulation, control systems, computer vision) and firmware development.
What signal types are common in robotics?
Robotics startups show Engineering Hiring Bursts as they scale software teams for autonomy and perception systems. Infrastructure Buildouts signal investment in simulation and testing infrastructure.
What stages of robotics are tracked?
The dataset covers Pre-seed through Series A/B and Growth-stage robotics companies. Both hardware-first and software-first robotics startups are included.
Which geographies are represented?
Robotics startups span APAC (particularly Japan), EU, and US markets. The dataset includes both established industrial robotics companies and emerging service robotics startups.
How is robotics different from other hardware sectors?
Robotics companies tend to have substantial software engineering components (control systems, perception, simulation) that generate rich GitHub activity data, unlike traditional hardware-only companies.
Can I use this for competitive intelligence in robotics?
Yes. Monitor which robotics companies are accelerating their software engineering — often a leading indicator of product development progress or upcoming fundraises.
What are the most active robotics sub-sectors?
Industrial robotics software, medical robotics platforms, and autonomous navigation systems show the strongest signal density in the current dataset.
