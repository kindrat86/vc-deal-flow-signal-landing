State of Startup Engineering — Q3 2026

Data-driven analysis of engineering acceleration across 313 startups in 15 sectors

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Key Findings

  • The Q3 2026 dataset tracks 313 startups across 15 sectors, with an average 14-day commit velocity of 47.5 commits.
  • Gaming leads in tracked startup count with 35 startups, followed by Social & Community (29) and Web3 (28).
  • The dataset identifies four signal types: Deploy frequency spikes (55), Engineering hiring bursts (67), Infrastructure buildouts (24), and Framework migrations (167).
  • Top startups by velocity change show increases exceeding +999%, indicating rapid engineering acceleration in healthcare, edtech, and web3 sectors.
  • Engineering acceleration from public GitHub activity has historically preceded fundraise announcements by 3-6 weeks.

Methodology

Data Sources: GitHub API v3 — search/repositories, stats/commit_activity, contributors endpoints. Queried weekly across sector-specific topic clusters. Excludes large tech companies, major open-source foundations, and non-startup organizations.
Core Metrics: Commit Velocity (14-day rolling total), Commit Velocity Change (% vs. prior window), Contributor Count & Growth (6-week comparison), New Repositories (30-day window).
Signal Classification: Engineering hiring burst (contributor growth >50%), Infrastructure buildout (3+ new repos in 30 days), Deploy frequency spike (commit velocity +150%+), Framework migration (general acceleration).
Stage Estimation: Pre-seed (1-7 contributors), Seed (8-19), Series A/B (20-49), Growth (50+).
