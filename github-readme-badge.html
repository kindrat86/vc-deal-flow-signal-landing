Add a Velocity Badge to Your GitHub README

Show off your startup's engineering velocity with a free, auto-updating SVG badge. It takes 30 seconds.

Engineering velocity is a signal of startup health and momentum. By adding a GitDealFlow badge to your repository's README, you transparently share your team's commitment pace and contributor growth with everyone who visits your repo — investors, contributors, and customers alike.

The badge auto-updates every Monday with fresh data from our engineering velocity dataset, which tracks 369 startups across 15 sectors.

Step-by-Step Guide

1 Find Your GitHub Organization Name

Your GitHub organization name is the part after github.com/ in your org URL. For example, if your org URL is https://github.com/vercel, your org name is vercel.

2 Copy the Badge Markdown

Copy the markdown code below, replacing {startup-name} with your GitHub org name:

[![momentum](https://signals.gitdealflow.com/api/badge/{startup-name})](https://gitdealflow.com/github-readme-badge)

For example, for medplum:

[![momentum](https://signals.gitdealflow.com/api/badge/medplum)](https://gitdealflow.com/github-readme-badge)

3 Paste into Your README.md

Open your project's README.md, paste the markdown where you'd like the badge to appear (typically near the top alongside other status badges), and commit.

Badge Customization

You can customize the badge appearance by appending query parameters:

ParameterOptionsDefaultDescription
themedark, light, neon, minimaldarkColor theme for the badge
styleflat, flat-square, plasticflatBadge shape/style
sizesmall, medium, largemediumOverall badge size

Example: https://signals.gitdealflow.com/api/badge/medplum?theme=light&style=flat-square

Why Add a Badge?

  • Social proof — Show visitors and potential contributors that your project is actively maintained and growing
  • Transparency — Openly share your team's engineering velocity as a signal of health and momentum
  • Benchmark awareness — Help your community understand where your project stands in terms of engineering activity
  • Investor signal — Give angel investors and VCs an immediate sense of your team's shipping velocity
  • Recruitment — Engineers want to join teams that ship. Your velocity badge proves you do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the velocity badge free?

Yes, the badge is completely free. It auto-updates weekly with the latest engineering velocity data from our public dataset.

Does the badge show real-time data?

The badge updates weekly (every Monday) with the latest commit velocity, contributor count, and signal type data from our dataset.

Can I customize the badge colors?

Yes, our badge API supports multiple color themes including dark, light, neon, and minimal. See the Badge Customization section above for available options and examples.

Do I need an API key?

No, the badge API is public and free. No authentication or API key is required.

Which startups are tracked?

GitDealFlow tracks 369 venture-backed startups across 15 sectors including Healthcare, EdTech, E-commerce Infrastructure, Enterprise SaaS, Web3, and more. Check signals.gitdealflow.com for the full searchable list.
