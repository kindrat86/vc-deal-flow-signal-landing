Why Berlin?

Berlin has established itself as continental Europe's premier startup ecosystem, with particular strength in B2B SaaS, fintech, mobility, and deep tech. The city's relatively low cost of living (compared to London or Paris) attracts international engineering talent, while its rich history in hardware and manufacturing feeds into Industry 4.0 and robotics ventures. Home to unicorns like N26, Trade Republic, and Flix, Berlin's ecosystem benefits from strong government support, a vibrant investor community, and the spillover effects of the region's automotive and industrial base.
