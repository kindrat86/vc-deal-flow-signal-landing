About GitDealFlow

Last updated: July 29, 2026

GitDealFlow is an open dataset and analytics platform tracking startup engineering velocity from public GitHub activity.

We track 324 startups across 15 sectors, measuring commit velocity, contributor growth, new repository creation, and signal patterns. Updated weekly. Dataset published under CC BY 4.0.
