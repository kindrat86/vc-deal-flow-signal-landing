Engineering Velocity in Startups: How to Measure, Track, and Read the Signal

Published 2026-07-30 · By The Data Nerd · 11 min read

Engineering velocity — how quickly a team turns ideas into shipped code — is the single most underutilized signal in venture capital. GitDealFlow tracks this across 369 startups in real time.

What Is Engineering Velocity?

Engineering velocity measures the rate at which a development team delivers working software. In startup investing, we track three sub-signals:

Commit Velocity

Number of commits per 14-day window. Current high: fleetbase (17).

Contributor Growth

Change in active contributor count = team expansion. fleetbase: ++73%.

Repository Expansion

New repos indicate infrastructure investment. aphp: 3 new repos.
