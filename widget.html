Frequently Asked Questions

Is the widget free?

Yes, completely free. No attribution required (though appreciated). No rate limits. No account needed.

How often does the data update?

The widget refreshes every Monday ~09:00 UTC when we run the weekly signal sweep. The data is cached and updated in real-time from the API.

Can I customize the widget?

The default widget shows top 5 trending startups. You can filter by sector by adding ?sector=fintech to the iframe URL. Contact us for custom integrations.
