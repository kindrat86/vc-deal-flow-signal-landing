About Startups in India

India is APAC's largest startup ecosystem outside China, with Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, and Mumbai as major hubs. Indian engineering teams are prolific open-source contributors, especially in fintech, e-commerce infrastructure, and enterprise SaaS.

The Asia-Pacific region is home to some of the world's fastest-growing startup ecosystems. From India's massive engineering workforce to Singapore's concentrated innovation hub, APAC startups are increasingly dominant contributors to global open-source infrastructure, e-commerce, and fintech.

Why Track India Startups

Engineering velocity is a predictive indicator of startup momentum. By tracking commit velocity, contributor growth, and infrastructure buildout patterns, investors can identify which Indian startups are accelerating 3–6 weeks before public announcements. GitDealFlow monitors public GitHub activity across 24 Indian organizations, providing weekly-updated signals.
