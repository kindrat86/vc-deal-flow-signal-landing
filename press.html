About GitDealFlow

GitDealFlow is a deal-flow signal tool for investors (not a fund) that reads startups' public GitHub engineering activity to flag the ones accelerating 21–47 days before the round. The platform tracks 356 startup GitHub organizations across 15 sectors, computing six engineering velocity variables weekly. All data is publicly accessible via a free API, CSV/JSON downloads, an MCP server, and an A2A endpoint. The underlying research is published as an SSRN preprint (abstract 6606558).

Data Methodology

The full methodology is published at signals.gitdealflow.com/methodology. In brief:

  • Startup GitHub organizations are identified from Crunchbase, Wellfound, and PitchBook fundraise data
  • Six metrics are computed weekly from public commit logs, contributor activity, and repository growth
  • A composite Engineering Momentum Score (0–100) combines all signals using a proprietary weighting
  • Breakout acceleration is defined as top 5% week-over-week momentum gain
  • Historical backtesting shows a 21–47 day lead time between acceleration detection and public fundraise or press announcements
  • The research methodology is documented and peer-reviewed via SSRN preprint

Frequently Asked Questions for Journalists

What makes GitDealFlow different from Crunchbase or PitchBook?
GitDealFlow uses engineering activity — commits, contributors, and repos — as a leading indicator of startup momentum, rather than relying on self-reported company data or announced fundraises. The signal is forward-looking, not historical.
How accurate is the acceleration signal?
Historical backtesting shows acceleration detected 21–47 days before public fundraise announcements. The methodology is published as an SSRN preprint with full transparency on false positive rates and signal thresholds.
Is the data free for journalists to use?
Yes. The full dataset is available under CC BY 4.0. Journalists can download CSV/JSON exports or use the API — no registration required. Attribution to GitDealFlow and a link to the SSRN preprint is appreciated.
Can I get early access to data for a story?
Contact signals@gitdealflow.com for press inquiries and early data access for editorial coverage.
Who runs GitDealFlow?
GitDealFlow is operated by an independent, pseudonymous team. The creator goes by "The Data Nerd" for public-facing work. Contact signals@gitdealflow.com for direct inquiries.
Are there case studies or real-world examples?
Yes. The methodology page includes case studies of startups whose acceleration was detected before fundraise announcements. Visit signals.gitdealflow.com/methodology for details.
