Detailed breakdown

GitDealFlow

Best for: Real-time engineering momentum | Price: Free tier available

Daily-refreshed GitHub signals. Commits, contributors, stars — tracked across 400+ startups. No waiting for quarterly reports.

Crunchbase

Best for: Round-history database | Price: From $29/mo

Largest funding database. Good for 'who raised what' lookups. Same lag as CBI: data enters after rounds close.

PitchBook

Best for: Deal terms + fund performance | Price: From $20K+/yr

Deeper deal-terms data than CBI. Strong LP/fund coverage. Heavier, slower, more expensive.

Tracxn

Best for: Emerging market startup sourcing | Price: From $15K/yr

Excellent taxonomy. Multi-signal approach (hiring, news, engineering). Good complement to CBI for specific geographies.

Dealroom

Best for: European ecosystem mapping | Price: From $10K/yr

Strongest European coverage. Good for understanding ecosystem-level trends. Round-based, not pre-round.

Grata

Best for: B2B company search + sourcing | Price: From $12K/yr

Massive B2B graph. Good for finding companies that don't appear in CBI. Not a signal tool.

Our verdict: CB Insights is unmatched for sector-level research and quarterly briefings. But it doesn't tell you what's happening this week. GitDealFlow does — and at 1/500th the cost.

Frequently asked questions

What does CB Insights miss?

CB Insights excels at structured market intelligence — sector reports, patent analysis, earnings-call transcripts. But it does not track real-time code velocity or contributor growth on GitHub. Those signals are what predict a fundraise, and CBI only picks up the story after the round closes.

Is CB Insights obsolete?

Far from it. For board-ready market sizing, competitive landscape mapping, and industry thesis development, CBI is best-in-class. The gap is in speed-to-signal — and that's where GitDealFlow fits.

Can I use GitDealFlow alongside CBI?

This is the ideal stack. CBI for quarterly research and board decks. GitDealFlow for the weekly 'what should I look at' question. The two tools answer different time horizons.
