Frequently asked questions

What's Tracxn's weakness?

Tracxn aggregates signals from hiring, news, web traffic, and engineering — which is comprehensive. But each signal source has its own lag, and analyst curation adds further delay. A startup can be accelerating for weeks before the Tracxn profile updates.

Is GitDealFlow better than Tracxn?

They answer different questions. Tracxn tells you 'what's happening in X sector across all signal types.' GitDealFlow tells you 'which 5 startups accelerated on GitHub this week.' For speed-to-signal, GitDealFlow wins. For sector landscape reports, Tracxn wins.
