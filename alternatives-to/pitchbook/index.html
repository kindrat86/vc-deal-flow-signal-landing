Detailed breakdown

GitDealFlow

Best for: Pre-round GitHub engineering signal | Price: Free tier available

Detects acceleration 3-6 weeks before fundraise announcements. Tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, star acceleration across 400+ startup orgs. EUR 9.97/month — 1/2000th the cost of PitchBook.

Crunchbase

Best for: Investor profiles + funding rounds | Price: From $29/mo

Largest startup database. Good for confirming rounds and mapping investor networks. Same limitation: rounds appear after they close.

CB Insights

Best for: Sector intelligence + earnings analysis | Price: From $40K/yr

Unmatched sector depth. Patents, earnings calls, M&A rumors. Excellent for thesis development, not for daily deal flow.

Tracxn

Best for: Taxonomy-driven startup discovery | Price: From $15K/yr

The best sector taxonomy in the market. Strong in India/SEA. Uses multiple signals including hiring, news, and engineering.

Affinity

Best for: CRM + relationship intelligence | Price: From $149/mo

Relationship-mapping CRM, not a signal tool. Shows who in your network can intro you to a startup, but doesn't find the startup.

Source Scrub

Best for: Bootstrapped company sourcing | Price: From $10K/yr

Strong on bootstrapped companies that don't appear in Crunchbase. Conference-driven data, not live code signal.

Frequently asked questions

Why would I need something besides PitchBook?

PitchBook is a lagging indicator — it records deals that already happened. If you want to find startups before they raise, you need a leading indicator. GitDealFlow tracks engineering acceleration, which reliably precedes fundraise announcements by 3-6 weeks.

Does GitDealFlow replace PitchBook for LP reporting?

No. PitchBook is still the standard for LP reporting, deal-term benchmarking, and fund performance data. Keep it. Add GitDealFlow for the sourcing layer.

What's the cost difference?
