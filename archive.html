Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe to the weekly digest?
Visit /subscribe and enter your email. It's free, no credit card required, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
When is the weekly digest sent?
Every Sunday at 14:00 UTC. It covers engineering acceleration signals detected during the prior week.
How are the five startups selected each week?
The five startups with the highest engineering momentum score gains for the week — the top breakout signals across all 15 sectors. Read the full methodology at signals.gitdealflow.com/methodology.
Can I access older issues beyond what's shown here?
Archives back to January 2025 are available at signals.gitdealflow.com/archive. This page shows the most recent issues.
Can I share an issue with my team?
Absolutely. Each issue has a share button. You can also forward the email or share the direct link to any issue.
