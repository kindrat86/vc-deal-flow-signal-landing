Frequently Asked Questions About Gaming Startups

How does GitDealFlow track gaming startups?
Gaming engineering velocity is measured from public GitHub activity across game engines, developer tools, and gaming infrastructure platforms. The focus is on the software engineering that powers modern game development.
What signal types are most common in gaming?
Deploy Frequency Spikes dominate as game engine and tooling teams ship continuously. Engineering Hiring Bursts appear during major platform or engine version development cycles.
What stages of gaming startups are tracked?
The dataset covers Pre-seed through Growth-stage gaming companies, including game engine developers, publishing platforms, and gaming middleware providers.
Which geographies are represented?
Gaming startups span US, APAC, and global markets. The dataset reflects the international nature of game development.
How can gaming investors use velocity data?
Track which gaming infrastructure companies are accelerating engineering investment — a leading indicator of platform maturity, community growth, and fundraise timing.
What sub-sectors of gaming are covered?
The dataset covers game engines and frameworks, developer tooling, publishing and distribution platforms, and gaming analytics infrastructure.
Does velocity in gaming correlate with publisher interest?
Yes. Gaming infrastructure velocity spikes often precede publishing platform expansions, engine version releases, or fundraises by 21–47 days, consistent with the broader dataset.
