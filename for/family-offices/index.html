For family offices: early-stage venture signal without the overhead

Family offices allocating to venture want access to early deals without building a full deal-flow team. GitDealFlow provides a pre-round signal that surfaces startups accelerating on GitHub, 21–47 days before the round — no team of analysts required.

The problem: Family offices often rely on fund-of-funds, co-investment relationships, or direct allocations through networks. Building proprietary deal flow is expensive and most signal tools are priced for institutions.

How GitDealFlow fits

GitDealFlow reads GitHub engineering activity across 4,200+ startup orgs and sends five accelerating startups every Sunday — each with sector, stage, and a plain-English note. The Dashboard adds ranked filters for €9.97/month, far below institutional tooling.

Where the proof lives

Methodology published as SSRN preprint 6606558. The lead time (21–47 days pre-round) has been validated against 219 documented fundraises.

Pricing & tiers: Free Signal Digest. Dashboard €9.97/month. Insider Circle €97/month for live briefings and the private Telegram group.

How it fits your stack

Pair with PitchBook for valuation/diligence on specific targets. GitDealFlow is the discovery layer.

Common questions

Is GitDealFlow a fund we can invest in?

No. GitDealFlow is a tool, not a fund and not an investor. It surfaces signal; you make the allocations.

Is it suitable for non-technical allocators?

Yes. Each startup comes with a plain-English note on why it's accelerating. No code-reading required.
