For private equity analysts

PE analysts need to track portfolio company momentum and spot add-on targets. GitDealFlow’s engineering signal provides a quantitative, non-financial growth indicator that is often visible weeks before revenue data.

Why PE analysts use GitDealFlow

PE analysts track portfolio company health via financials, but financials lag. Engineering acceleration — rising commit velocity, contributor growth, repo expansion — is visible weeks earlier. For software-heavy portfolio companies and add-on acquisition targets with public GitHub orgs, GitDealFlow provides a leading indicator of whether the team is building, scaling, or stalling.

Two use cases

Portfolio monitoring: track the engineering velocity of software portfolio companies. A plateauing signal before a revenue stall is an early warning; an accelerating signal before a growth quarter is a leading confirmation.

Add-on discovery: watch for accelerating startups in adjacent sectors — candidates for bolt-on acquisitions before they’re marketed by bankers.

Common questions

Does this work for non-software portfolio companies?

Partial. It works best for portfolio companies with public GitHub activity — which skews toward software, SaaS, and tech-enabled businesses. It’s weaker for hard assets, manufacturing, or services.

How is this different from Grata or SourceScrub?

Grata and SourceScrub are PE-specific target search engines. GitDealFlow is a momentum layer — it tells you which targets are accelerating, not just which ones exist. See /vs/grata and /vs/source-scrub for full comparisons.
