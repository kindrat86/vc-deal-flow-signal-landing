Deal memo template (free, copy-paste)

A clean deal memo template for early-stage investors — the exact sections to capture when evaluating a startup, including where to record engineering-momentum signals. Free to copy and adapt.

Free to copy and adapt. All GitDealFlow templates are released for the investor community.

What this template is for

A deal memo is the one-page document you write after a first meeting. Its job is to force clarity: does this startup fit the thesis, is the team real, is the signal there, and should it advance? This template captures the essentials without bloat.

Template fields

  • Company name, sector, stage, geography
  • Founder(s) + key team
  • Source of the deal (GitDealFlow digest / network / inbound / referral)
  • Signal snapshot (engineering momentum, traction, hiring)
  • Thesis fit (1–5)
  • Round details (size, valuation, lead, timeline)
  • Key risks
  • Decision (advance / pass / monitor) + next step

How to use it

Most investors keep this in Notion or Google Docs per portfolio company. The 'signal snapshot' field is where GitDealFlow users paste the engineering-acceleration note — it standardizes the pre-round signal across deals so you can compare momentum objectively.

Common questions

Is this template free?

Yes. All templates on this site are free to copy and adapt.

Can I customize it?

Absolutely — adapt the fields and sections to your fund's workflow. The structure is a starting point, not a constraint.

Where does the GitDealFlow signal fit?

In the 'signal snapshot' field — paste the weekly digest note for the startup. That standardizes the pre-round momentum signal across all your memos.
