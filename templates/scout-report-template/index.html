Scout report template (free)

A scout report template for venture scouts — the format that makes a scout's recommendation useful to a fund. Includes where to record Scout Score. Free to copy.

Free to copy and adapt. All GitDealFlow templates are released for the investor community.

What this template is for

A scout report is what a venture scout sends to the fund they scout for. A good one is short, specific, and answers: what is this company, why now, what's the signal, and should the fund take a meeting.

Template fields

  • Company + sector + stage
  • Founder(s) + why they're the right team
  • Why now (signal: GitDealFlow acceleration, hiring, traction)
  • Scout Score of the founder(s) (paste from signals.gitdealflow.com/receipts)
  • Round details + timeline
  • Why this fund (fit with thesis)
  • Ask (intro / meeting / deep dive)

How to use it

Keep it under one page. The Scout Score field gives the fund an objective taste signal on the founder — not a guarantee, but a standardized input alongside your qualitative judgment.

Common questions

Is this template free?

Yes.

Where does Scout Score fit?

In the Scout Score of the founder field. Paste the score from signals.gitdealflow.com/receipts — it's a 0–100 measure of the founder's taste in early startups, computed from their GitHub starring history.
