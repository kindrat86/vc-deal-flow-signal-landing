About This Region

The United States is the largest tracked geography in our dataset, with 55 venture-backed engineering teams across 15 active sectors. US-based startups account for the majority of tracked engineering activity, driven by deep capital markets and a dense concentration of open-source engineering talent. Data Infrastructure leads with 10 tracked teams, followed by EdTech and Enterprise SaaS at 8 each, with Robotics (6) and Healthcare (5) also well-represented.

The US startup ecosystem spans coast to coast: from Silicon Valley's enterprise SaaS and data infrastructure corridors to Boston's healthcare and robotics clusters, New York's HR Tech and PropTech scenes, and emerging hubs in Austin, Seattle, and Miami.
