Framework Migration — Startup Signal

Framework migration signals — how moving from one tech stack to another reveals startup maturity and future engineering

A framework migration signal detects when a startup undergoes a technology stack transformation. This is a dual-indicator signal: short-term velocity may dip during migration, but 6-week forward velocity typically shows 2-3x acceleration post-migration. Framework migrations signal engineering maturity, technical debt reduction, and architectural scaling for product growth. The most commonly detected migrations include: Next.js adoption, React-to-Vue transitions, and database scaling.
