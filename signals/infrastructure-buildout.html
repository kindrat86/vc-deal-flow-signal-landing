Infrastructure Buildout — Startup Signal

Infrastructure buildout signals in startups — new repos, microservice creation, and architecture modernization as indica

An infrastructure buildout signal is triggered when a startup creates new repositories, restructures codebases into microservices, or migrates critical infrastructure. This is the strongest predictor of sustained engineering velocity — companies showing infrastructure buildout signals maintain acceleration for 8+ weeks. Common triggers: monolith-to-microservice migration, new cloud provider adoption, deployment infrastructure modernization, and test/QA pipeline investments.
