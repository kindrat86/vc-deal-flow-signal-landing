About This Region

Europe is our second-largest tracked region with 46 venture-backed engineering teams across 14 active sectors. The European startup ecosystem spans major tech hubs including London, Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, Stockholm, and Barcelona, with strong representation in Supply Chain (7 teams), Healthcare (6), EdTech (6), and E-commerce Infrastructure (6).

European startups show a distinct profile compared to the US: a higher proportion of pre-seed and seed stage teams (24 of 46), indicating a vibrant early-stage funding environment. Framework Migration signals dominate (20 teams), suggesting active technology stack modernization across the continent. Supply Chain and logistics tech is a European strength, with 7 tracked teams including nmi-agro, sema-project, and openfoodfarms. Germany, France, and the Netherlands are the most represented countries.

Why Engineering Velocity Matters in Europe

For VCs and deal sourcers tracking the European market, engineering velocity signals provide a critical leading indicator that traditional fundraising data cannot match. By monitoring commit cadence, contributor growth, and signal type distribution across 46 teams, investors can identify which startups are accelerating real engineering output months before a formal fundraise.

European startups with high Framework Migration activity — 20 of 46 teams — are actively modernizing their technology stacks, often a precursor to scaling engineering teams and launching new products. The 10 Engineering Hiring Bursts detected across the region signal teams that are rapidly expanding headcount, while 7 Deploy Frequency Spikes indicate shipping acceleration. Combined, these signals create a rich dataset for detecting breakout companies across London, Berlin, Paris, and beyond.

GitDealFlow's methodology tracks all public GitHub repositories across each startup's organization, computing contributor counts, commit velocity over trailing 14-day windows, and signal type classification based on statistical deviations from baseline activity.
