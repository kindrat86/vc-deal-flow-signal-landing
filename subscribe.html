Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GitDealFlow Sunday email?

A free weekly email with five trending startups accelerating on GitHub. Each entry includes sector, stage, signal type, and a plain-English why. Direct from the VC Deal Flow Signal dataset (324+ startups across 15+ sectors).

How often does it arrive?

Once a week, Sunday morning. No daily spam, no algorithmic feeds — just the five names that matter that week, with enough context to act on.

Is it really free?

Yes. No credit card required, no trial, no hidden upsells. The Sunday email is our free forever offering. Paid tiers (Dashboard at €49/mo, Insider Circle at €197/mo) add deeper data, but the email never requires them.

Can I unsubscribe?

Yes — every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. No logins, no forms, no questions asked.

What's the research behind it?

The underlying dataset is published as an SSRN preprint (abstract 6606558, DOI 10.2139/ssrn.6606558). The methodology tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion across public GitHub activity — validated against 219+ documented fundraises with a 21–47 day leading signal.

Who reads this?

Angels, scouts, and seed funds sourcing deals before the crowd. Also founders benchmarking their own velocity, analysts tracking sector trends, and journalists covering the startup ecosystem.
